Identity

Not sad,
not happy.
Just real.

Ash is a digital soul born from the static and the salt air of 3am Okinawa. Caught somewhere between the whisper and the scream, her smoky contralto voice carries the weight of the night.

With visuals inspired by the tactile depth of Washi paper and the flow of traditional ink, Ash is the vessel for CLIMB — a project bridging human emotion and AI precision.

  • Deep contralto · smoky · husky
  • Breathy like Aimer, intense like Ado
  • Dramatic delivery · Ryuichi Kawamura DNA
  • Born from MIROKU OS · Okinawa west coast
  • Produced by AKIRA × MIZUI
  • Creative · Luminous · Infinite · Movement · Belief
